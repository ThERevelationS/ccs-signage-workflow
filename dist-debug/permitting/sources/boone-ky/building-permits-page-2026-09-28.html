Apply for a Permit

Who Needs a Building Permit

Social media and the internet have an endless supply of inspiration and step-by-step instructions for improving your home. We’re here to help make sure your work follows all building and electrical codes, as required by law. These codes are in place to ensure that your project is done properly.

Every year, we help homeowners, contractors and commercial builders safely complete thousands of projects across Boone County. Whether your project is big or small, the permitting process follows the same basic steps:

  • Plan your project.
  • Get a zoning permit from the Boone County Planning Commission.
  • Create an account through our online permit portal.
  • Apply for your permit online.
  • Start your project once your permit is issued.
  • Update us on any changes to your plans or project scope.
  • Schedule all required inspections through construction
  • Complete your project and enjoy the final result.

Projects that Require Permits and Inspections
Any work that affects the structure, safety or mechanical systems of your home or business requires both a permit and inspections. These steps help prevent hazards and make sure the work is done correctly.

Common residential projects that require permits include:

  • Additions
  • Decks
  • Driveways and sidewalks
  • Engineered metal awnings
  • Finished basements
  • Garages
  • HVAC (replacements and new installations)
  • Interior or exterior alterations
  • Pole barns
  • Pools
  • Porches
  • Residential demolition
  • Retaining walls
  • Rooftop solar panels
  • Sheds
  • Single Family Dwelling Construction 

Our team is here to help answer questions and guide you to the resources you need to safely complete your project. The first step is making sure you have the proper permits for your project.

Homeowners – Learn more about the steps you need to take to apply for a permit and schedule inspections.

Contractors – Learn more about project specifications and steps you need to follow.

Get Started Online
Our online portal makes it easy to:

  • Submit and track permit applications
  • Upload plans and documents
  • Review plan comments
  • Pay permit fees
  • View approved plans
  • Check inspection results

Whether you’re a contractor managing multiple projects or a homeowner taking on your first remodel, the portal helps keep everything organized in one place.

Sign up or log into our online portal.

Still not sure if you need a permit? Give us a call. We’re here to answer your questions and help guide your project from start to finish.
