How to Set Up an ezTrak Account for submitting Electrical Permits or Plan Review to
Inspection Bureau. Inc. (IBI) for Hamilton County and Cincinnati submittals only.

NOTE: For Kentucky submittals refer to the IBI Home Page

To submit permits/plans online through ezTrak for Hamilton County and the City of Cincinnati,
you’ll need to register for a CAGIS ezTrak membership. This gives you access to apply for
permits/plans, track projects, and receive notifications.

We recommend looking over the ezTrak Account Registration page, https://eztrak.cagis.org/
prior to starting your registration.

1. Go to the Registration Page

Visit the official registration page: CAGIS ezTrak
Registration


2. Provide Your Registration Details

You’ll need:

      User Name: Your name or the name of the business you represent.

      Email Address: Your own active email account (CAGIS does not provide one). This will
       be your login ID and is used for all communications. We highly recommend using a
       generic email address that is not tied to a specific person like
       permits@companyname.com.

      Password: At least 8 characters, including at least one number, no special characters,
       and case-sensitive.

3. Complete the Registration

      Click Register to create your account.

      You’ll receive a confirmation email; check your inbox and spam folder.

      Once registered, log in to access the Apply for Permits section.

4. Prepare for Your Permit Application

Before starting:

      Review the specific instructions for the permit or plan review submittal, including
       fees. (IBI Fee Schedule)
      Ensure your email is active, as you’ll receive important updates and notifications.

5. Start Applying Online

From the ezTrak home page:

      Use the Start an Application link or the New tab in the top toolbar to begin.

      Follow the step-by-step process, providing project, owner, contractor, and work type
       details.

      Save your application and proceed to fee payment and/or document submission.

6. Track Your Application

      Use your Application number to track your application in the “Record” tab using “My
       Records” or the “Action Center”.

      IBI’s application numbers are identified by “IBI-PMT” for electrical permits and with
       “IBI-RVW” for plan review. Jurisdiction identifiers are as follows: “C” (Cincinnati
       Building Department), “H” (Hamilton County Building Department) and “0” (Other
       Municipality Building Departments)

      You can also set up email notifications to be alerted about updates.

Tip: If you have multiple employees or partners, set up your account to allow shared access
and collaboration
