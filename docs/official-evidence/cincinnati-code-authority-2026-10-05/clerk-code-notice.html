Notice To Online Users

This on-line version of the Cincinnati Municipal Code, related legislation, and regulations is not the official version. The only officially published version is available through the office of the Clerk of Council, located in Room 308 of City Hall, phone 513-352-3246. This on-line version may, occasionally, differ from the official version and should be relied upon for general information purposes only.
