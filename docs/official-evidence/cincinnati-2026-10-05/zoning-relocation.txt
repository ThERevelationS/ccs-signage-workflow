Relocation of Zoning Division to the City Planning and Engagement Department
RELOCATION OF ZONING DIVISION TO THE CITY PLANNING AND ENGAGEMENT DEPARTMENT
Effective October 3, 2021, the Zoning Division will be relocated to the City Planning and Engagement Department on the 7th floor, Centennial II, 805 Central Avenue. The Permit Center in the Department of Buildings & Inspections (B&I) will continue to coordinate all building permit applications. We ask that all permit applications received at the Permit Center, or by mail be submitted with 4 sets of plans.
This will facilitate the Zoning Division’s ability to simultaneously review plans at the same time as the Building Plan Examiner. Once a building permit application has been submitted and assigned a building permit number, the Permit Center will route plans to all necessary approval agencies, including the Zoning Division.
Our online permits services remain the best way to have your permit processed. We encourage you to submit your application by using Electronic Plan Submission (which will eliminate 4 sets of plans), online application (plumbing and HVAC) or by mail, 805 Central Avenue, Suite 500, Cincinnati, Ohio 45202. For questions about applications, please contact Customer Service at (513) 352-3271, opt. 9.
If you have any questions or need assistance, please call 513-352-3271, Option 9 or email CCPBPERMITCENTER@cincinnati-oh.gov.
