Development & Zoning
Most of Green Township falls under the jurisdiction of Hamilton County zoning regulations. Green Township's own zoning regulations govern a portion of northeastern Green Township.
For properties within Green Township's zoning jurisdiction, use the Zoning Street Directory below to search by street name or address and find the zoning designation and what the property is zoned for.
For properties outside Northeast Green Township's zoning jurisdiction:
Visit Hamilton County Planning + Development for zoning information and assistance. Zoning, building, and development permit applications are available through the CAGIS ezTrak Portal.
