Zoning Department

Planning for sound future development while preserving our rural character.

About the Zoning Department

The Zoning Department was established to plan, create goals for land use development, and oversee the future growth of Crosby Township. As the Township attracts more residents, it is necessary to preserve the atmosphere that makes Crosby Township a pleasant place to live.

The Zoning Resolution was originally adopted by the Trustees in November 1974. The purpose of the resolution is to promote public health, safety, morals, comfort, and general welfare; to conserve and protect property and property values; to secure the most appropriate use of land; and to facilitate adequate but economical provision of public improvements, all in accordance with Chapter 519 of the Ohio Revised Code.

Zoning Boards

Board of Zoning Appeals

The Board of Zoning Appeals meets on an as-needed basis. They are empowered to hear variance requests, conditional use requests, appeals, and requests for changes in non-conforming uses.

Members: Dennis Kautz, Bill Weisbrodt, Ed Telscher, Bryon Lewis, Jim Taphorn, Sarah Cooper

Zoning Commission

The Zoning Commission holds public meetings to develop and submit a recommended plan of zoning in the unincorporated area of the Township. They are also responsible for amending and updating the zoning plan.

Members: Gordon Wong, Jennifer Snyder, Gwen Lawhorn, Dennis Simpson, Greg White, Ed Losekamp
