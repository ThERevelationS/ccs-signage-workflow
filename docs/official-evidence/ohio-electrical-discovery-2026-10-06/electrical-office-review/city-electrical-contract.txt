Inspections Related To Building Permits, And Construction
Inspections related to building permits, and construction such as soil, framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and final is responsible for performing all field inspections on residential and commercial construction projects. The Inspection section also serves the community by administering the Contractor Registration program, provides building safety education programs to local schools and community groups, instructs continuing education courses for the local trade councils and performs the following license inspections:
Daycare: Child and Adult
Licenses: Liquor, Cabaret, Dance Hall and Theater, Parking Lot, Second Hand Auto and Motor Vehicle Salvage
To schedule an inspection pertaining to your specific project, please call the inspector listed on the front of your building, plumbing, or mechanical permit.
The City contracts with Inspections Bureau, Inc. or IBI to review and inspect all electrical permits. Visit www.inspectionbureau.com to learn more.
To schedule an inspection not pertaining to a permit, call 513-352-3275.
