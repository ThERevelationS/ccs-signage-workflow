Zoning Info
Village council recently approved a new zoning ordinance. Hamilton County will provide assistance with all zoning related issues. All building permits will be issued by The Hamilton County Building Department. Copies of the new Zoning Booklet will be available at the village office for a $10.00 fee. For more information contact our Deputy Clerk in the office at 941-0610. The Zoning Code is available as a PDF download. Click on the link below to download.
