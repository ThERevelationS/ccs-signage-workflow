      FIRM, FIS, LOMR, LOMA Information

      Staff provides basic floodplain information including current Flood Insurance Rates Maps (FIRM), Flood Insurance Study (FIS), Letters of Map Revision (LOMR) and Letters of Map Amendment (LOMA) are on file with this office. This information allows us to provide you with the following specific information regarding your property:

        • Proximity to special flood hazards areas including floodways.
        • Base flood elevation, when determined by the study.
        • Estimated inundation data based on the ground contour (where available).

      In addition, for insurance purposes, we can provide information from the Flood Insurance Rate Map (FIRM) including community number, panel number, suffix, index date, elevation datum. Please contact our office for additional information.
