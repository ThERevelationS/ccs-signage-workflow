Planning & Zoning

Zoning Certificates/Permit Information

Zoning Certificate Application 

Beginning January 1, 2026, building permits and inspections for the Village of St. Bernard will be issued by the Hamilton County Building Department via their CAGIS ezTRak portal website. Visit their Hamilton County website or call 513-946-4550 for further information. Information on permits completed by the Village before January 1, 2026, can still be accessed through the St. Bernard portal website. If you have any questions regarding past permits, please call 513-242-7770.

The Village of St. Bernard Zoning Code regulates how property can be used and where structures can be placed on property. St. Bernard is divided into zoning districts, and each district has its own set of regulations on projects such as buildings, fences, accessory structures, and signs. Zoning in St. Bernard is managed and enforced by the Village Zoning Administrator.

All new buildings, residences, additions, signs, decks, fences, and accessory structures constructed within the Village of St. Bernard require an approved zoning certificate before work may begin and, in most instances, before applying for a building permit through Hamilton County.

Zoning Certificate Information Sheet (PDF) 

Applications can be completed via our iWorQ web portal. 

  • A Site Plan must accompany the application that shows direction (north standard), scale size, adjacent street(s), and the following features:
    • Dimensions of the lot/parcel
    • Location and size of driveways
    • Dimensions of buildings (existing and proposed)
    • Location of well and septic system
    • Setback distance of building from lot lines
    • Distance between buildings
  • A great resource for this information can be located at: CAGIS Online Website
  • Upon receipt of the application, the St. Bernard Zoning Administrator will review the application. An invoice will be emailed to the applicant. The fee for all zoning certificates is $50 and can be paid through the village portal linked on the invoice or via check to the Village of St. Bernard.
  • After payment is received, the approved zoning certificate will be emailed to the applicant.
  • Any additional building permits needed will be issued by the Hamilton County Building Department Website. Hamilton County will not issue a building permit for a structure until they have a copy of the approved Zoning Certificate.

For questions about zoning, please contact the St. Bernard Zoning Administrator at 513-482-5684.
