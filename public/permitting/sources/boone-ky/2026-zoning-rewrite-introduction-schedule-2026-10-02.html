The Boone County Planning Commission has begun the update of the Boone County Zoning Regulations.  The current version of the zoning regulations was adopted in late 2022/early 2023.  A website has been created for the update process.  It will allow the public to review the proposed changes in real time.

The overall project is anticipated to be adopted in late 2026.  Proposed text will be shared on this website allowing for public comment.

General Timeline

February 2026 - July 2026: Staff research and text preparation.

March 2026 - August 2026:  Planning Commission's Long Range/Comp Plan Committee evaluates proposed changes.

July 2026 - September 2026:  Public review and comment.

July 2026:  Stakeholder Meetings and Public Workshop.

September 2026 - October 2026: Adoption process including public hearing and action before the Planning Commission.

October 2026 - December 2026:  Adoption by the legislative bodies.

If you would like to provide input, you may do so by entering a Comment online.
