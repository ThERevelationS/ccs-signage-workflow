COMMISSION MEETINGS

Union, as an incorporated city, holds business meetings on a regular basis as required by law.

Regular Commission meetings are held on the 1st Monday of every month and Caucus meetings are held on the 3rd Monday of every month at 6:00 pm in the Union City Building, 1843 Bristow Drive, Union, KY, unless otherwise posted.

Meetings on calendar dates that conflict with Federal Holidays will be rescheduled.  Meeting notices are posted on city social media sites and at the city building. 

View: MEETING RULES.

Public hearings are scheduled as needed.  Notice of such hearings is electronically published, by law, in the city’s local newspaper.

Below are links to copies of past meeting minutes. Minutes are published after approval following the next month’s Regular Commission meeting.

Official records are retained at the City Building and may be viewed and/or copied by Open Records Request.
